A DIAMOND LEGACY OF LEARNING


At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, each child is nurtured in the holistic method of Dr. Maria Montessori.

Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines!

We are a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school, offering a viable alternative to international schools. At OBMC, we blend tradition and innovation to prepare a child for life.

Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing and productive individuals who can succeed, direct, and influence the future of an ever-changing world.

Our programs and discover the vibrant community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to grow and develop.


Campuses

Our campuses offer accessible, high-quality Montessori education in a nurturing environment for learning, creativity, and development.

Come and visit our facilities to experience what each OBMC campus can offer your child and family.



Fairview Campus

Our Fairview Campus in Quezon City delivers quality Montessori education to its thriving community of young families.

Greenhills Campus

Our Greenhills Campus in San Juan City offers its students a creative and fun environment to spark their imagination, love of learning, and service to the community.

Las Piñas Campus

Our Las Piñas Campus pays homage to the area's cultural history, the birthplace of the Sarao jeepney, and the unique 19th-century Bamboo Organ. Its welcoming atmosphere promotes the development and learning of students.



Sta. Ana Campus

Our Sta. Ana Campus is located in the heritage district of Manila. The school is known for its strong academic programs, community involvement, and love for culture and heritage.

Angeles Campus

Our Angeles Campus, located in the culturally rich and vibrant province of Pampanga, provides a nurturing and dynamic Montessori environment for students to thrive academically and personally.
